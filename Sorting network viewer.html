Sorting network viewer

Enter one layer per line. Each comparator is a pair of wire indices, and wires are numbered from 0 at the top.

Numbers on the left are positions. Numbers on the right follow each wire and show its original index. Comparators that end up upside down (their top end on a lower position than their bottom end) turn red. Untangle goes through the upside-down comparators from left to right: at each one the wires detach, then everything to its right swaps so the comparator points the right way. Step goes through the untangling one swap at a time, Back undoes the last swap, and Untangle plays whatever is left.
